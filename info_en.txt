VoxCompare - Hangazonosító 1.0.0

Compares two recordings and estimates whether the same person speaks in both.

  •  WAV, MP3, FLAC and OGG work without any external tool
  •  Silence removal and window-by-window analysis for a more accurate result
  •  Consistency check: warns when more than one voice is heard in a recording
  •  Hungarian and English UI; the model ships with the installer, no internet needed

All computation happens on your own computer; audio files are never uploaded.

Unofficial software. The result is an informative estimate, not a forensic expert opinion, and must not be used as evidence in legal proceedings; it is less reliable for short, noisy or heavily compressed recordings.
